Picks detail

Score = 35% heading quality (peaks at a true 45° NE arrow) + 25% step size + 25% tail % change + 15% sector strength, multiplied by a quadrant weight that favours early rotation (Improving ×1.30, Lagging ×1.20, Leading ×1.00, Weakening ×0.90), then rescaled so a flawless Improving name lands at exactly 100 and nothing clips into a tie.
